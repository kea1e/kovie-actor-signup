UARK theater students, thank you for helping bring Kovie's live roommate stories to life!

Kovie is a student-built roommate and housing platform through the McMillon Innovation Studio.

Choose one open half-hour slot and tap an open actor seat. It's held for you while you enter your name and UARK email. Sign up with a friend!

You'll get a confirmation email and a calendar invite. Short scripts will be emailed before the event. There will also be room for improvisation.

Questions or schedule changes? Contact Kate Carter at 832-773-8129.
